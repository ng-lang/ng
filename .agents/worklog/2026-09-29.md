**Defect found by the corpus:** a non-literal member value coerced into a
union slot was not boxed. Locals, call results, arithmetic, returns,
struct fields, enum payloads, and `:=` all passed a raw word where a tag box
was expected (segfault on the first tag read), and `true` landed in the i64
member of `bool | i64`. Fix: the checker records
`TypeCheckResult::unionCoercions` (expression → member type) in
`inferExpectedUnion`; FlowIR lowers the expression at its member type and
emits a new `WrapUnion` instruction; native lowering boxes it with
`unionMemberFor`.

**Verification:** SSA golden diff over all 61 examples shows only
`example/unions.ng` changed (bool literal now carries the bool tag). A
mutation check (forcing bool → i64 tag in WrapUnion) makes the PBT fail.
ng_test 656/656, ctest 657/657, ASan 656/656.
